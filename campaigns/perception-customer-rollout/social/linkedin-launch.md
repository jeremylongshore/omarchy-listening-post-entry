# LinkedIn Launch Post

Most AI news products compete to show you more.

I wanted one that knew when to show nothing.

So I built Perception: a private web signal room that watches a curated field of model releases, pricing changes, provider incidents, changelogs, and technical work.

The mechanism is simple:

1. Watch sources deliberately.
2. Rank changes against your topics.
3. Explain why each signal surfaced.
4. Keep the original source attached.
5. Let you finish the brief and return to work.

There is also a free Omarchy companion called Listening Post. It carries the actionable edge of the same field into the bar. A release or incident can tap you on the shoulder. Routine chatter stays quiet.

Perception is the paid web product. Listening Post is free and MIT licensed. The purchase email is the account, sign-in links work once, and paired-device credentials are revocable.

The working product: https://oma.intentsolutions.io/perception/

The companion and source: https://github.com/jeremylongshore/omarchy-listening-post-entry
