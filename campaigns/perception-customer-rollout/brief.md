# Perception Customer Rollout

- Status: rollout-gated
- Started: 2026-09-11
- Goal: Launch the complete paid Perception customer journey and use the free Listening Post companion as the Omarchy-native discovery and activation surface.
- Primary audience: Technical builders and operators using Omarchy who track AI releases, incidents, tools, and research.
- Primary angle: Ambient intelligence through the Perception Loop.
- Primary action: Buy Perception through Lemon Squeezy.
- Activation action: Sign in with the purchase email, keep or tune the starter topics, open a source-backed signal, then optionally pair Listening Post.
- Channels: Product website, Lemon Squeezy checkout, purchase email, GitHub, Omarchy community, Intent Solutions owned channels.
- Proof allowed: Working product interface, implemented source catalog, source links, verified behavior, free plugin code, local verification evidence.
- Proof forbidden: Invented customers, testimonials, traffic, revenue, conversion rates, or performance benchmarks.
- Open launch inputs: Price and billing interval, checkout URL, legal entity wording, refund window, support mailbox, production DNS, SMTP credentials, Lemon Squeezy product and webhook configuration.

## Funnel

Visitor → product mechanism → demo field → checkout → purchase-email access → first source-backed signal → topic confirmation → Listening Post pairing → returning brief.
