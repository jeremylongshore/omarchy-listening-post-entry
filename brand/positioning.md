# Perception Positioning

## Primary Angle: Ambient Intelligence

**Perception watches the web. Listening Post taps your shoulder when it matters.**

Perception is not another destination a customer must remember to check. The web app does the deep work—watching, ranking, explaining, and preserving sources—while Listening Post delivers only the actionable edge inside Omarchy.

## Unique Mechanism

The **Perception Loop**:

1. Watch a curated field of sources.
2. Rank changes against the customer's topics and operational impact.
3. Explain the few signals that cross the threshold.
4. Carry the actionable edge to Omarchy through Listening Post.
5. Return to the source-backed web brief for depth, then clear the field.

## Transformation

From repeatedly checking RSS readers, release pages, newsletters, status sites, and browser tabs to maintaining awareness while staying inside the work.

## Supporting Angles

- **Briefed, not fed:** a finite brief customers can understand, act on, and finish.
- **Explainable signal:** every signal has a reason and a source.
- **Built for keyboard operators:** an intelligence layer designed first for the Omarchy desktop.

## Competitive Boundary

Avoid saturated claims such as “cut through the noise,” “stay ahead,” “AI-powered summaries,” and “never miss an update.” Lead with the two-surface mechanism and the regained attention it makes possible.

## Launch Message Hierarchy

1. Stop checking feeds; keep working.
2. See the mechanism working with real source-backed signals.
3. Buy Perception for the private signal room.
4. Install Listening Post for quiet awareness in Omarchy.
5. Use the purchase email to sign in and pair the companion.
