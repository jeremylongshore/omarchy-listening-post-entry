# Perception Voice Profile

Perception sounds like a calm intelligence editor beside a technical operator: selective, observant, and precise. It reduces a noisy AI market to the few changes worth attention. Listening Post is the compressed form of that voice: factual, quiet, and brief.

## Personality

- **Discerning:** selection is the value.
- **Calm:** urgency comes from facts and hierarchy, never hype.
- **Technically honest:** state what happened, why it matters, where it came from, and when the field is stale.
- **Editorial:** briefs feel deliberately selected and arranged.
- **Protective of attention:** “nothing new” is a successful state.

## Voice Rules

- Lead with the outcome or change, not a rhetorical question.
- Use short and medium sentences with one decisive idea each.
- Prefer concrete verbs: shipped, changed, raised, cut, failed, recovered, opened, resolved.
- Name provenance, recency, and recovery when they matter.
- Use one signal-room metaphor at a time and anchor it in literal information.
- Perception may explain; Listening Post compresses to a count, status, source, and action.

## Vocabulary

Use: signal, field, brief, watchlist, source, priority, changed, shipped, incident, impact, last good field, connected, interrupted, worth your attention.

Avoid: revolutionary, game-changing, groundbreaking, supercharge, unlock, AI-powered insights, all-in-one dashboard, never miss anything, threat level, war room.

## Signature Language

- “What changed while you worked.”
- “Quiet by design.”
- “The source list is the product.”
- “Perception watches the web. Listening Post taps your shoulder when it matters.”

## Channel Adaptation

| Surface | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Product UI | Concise and state-driven: finding, evidence, action. |
| Landing page | Benefit-led and boldest, then mechanism and proof. |
| Email | Warmer: change, relevance, then one action. |
| Community launch | Editorial observation, mechanism, invitation. |
| Listening Post | A few words; interruption-aware. |

## Claims Boundary

Do not invent testimonials, metrics, customer counts, pricing, or completeness claims. Do not promise to watch everything. The defensible promise is a curated, source-backed field shaped by the customer's topics.
